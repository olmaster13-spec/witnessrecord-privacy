Witness app download: free on iPhone and Android

What it is, what it costs, which phones it runs on, and how to tell it from the other apps called Witness.

This page is for anyone who searched for the Witness app to download. It is the app for recording police, protests and immigration enforcement with the screen black and the footage uploaded to your own cloud while you film. On the App Store and Google Play it is listed as Witness: Camera Beyond Reach. It was called Witness Record until September 2026. Written by the developer of Witness Camera, so read it with that in mind; where another tool is a better fit, it says so.

Short answer. Witness Camera is free on iPhone (iOS 17 or later) and Android (11 or later), needs no account and has no Witness server. Black-screen recording, upload every 15 seconds to storage you own, SHA-256 hashing, a chain-of-custody manifest, independent time-stamping, emergency-contact sharing and 1080p dual-camera recording are all free. Witness Pro, $1.99 a month or $29.99 once on the US App Store, adds picture quality and extra outputs. Several unrelated apps are also called Witness; the list below tells them apart.

Where to download it

  • iPhone: the App Store, iOS 17 or later. Search for Witness: Camera Beyond Reach.
  • Android: Google Play, Android 11 or later, with the same name.
  • Computer: there is no desktop version. You do not need the app to check a recording someone sends you: the verification page does it in a browser.

What it costs

The app is free. Everything that protects footage is in the free app and stays free: black-screen recording, upload every 15 seconds to your own storage, SHA-256 hashing, the manifest, independent time-stamping, emergency-contact sharing and dual-camera recording at 1080p. There is no account, no subscription needed to record, and no advertising.

Witness Pro adds picture quality and some outputs and costs $1.99 a month or $29.99 once on the US App Store. For filming police or a protest you do not need it, because the free 1080p setting uploads fastest. Is Witness Pro worth it? says who should and should not pay.

What it does

  • Records with the screen fully black, or with a live preview if you prefer, and can use the front and rear cameras together.
  • Uploads footage in pieces of about 15 seconds to storage you own while you are still recording, so a seized or smashed phone costs you seconds, not everything.
  • Hashes every piece with SHA-256, writes a chain-of-custody manifest and gets it independently time-stamped, so a lawyer or newsroom can check that the footage has not been altered.
  • Shares your location and a link to the footage with an emergency contact you choose.
  • On iPhone, lets several phones film one event together with Witness Crew.
  • Each feature, and its limits, is explained in Witness features explained.

Which app called Witness is this?

The name is shared by several unrelated things, which is why searching for it returns a puzzle game, a charity and a security-camera app. This is the Witness app for recording police, protests and ICE encounters, listed on the stores as Witness: Camera Beyond Reach. If you meant another one, Witness App: which one do you actually mean? sorts them out and sends you to the right place. In short:

  • WITNESS, the human rights charity, is an organisation and not an app.
  • The Witness is a puzzle game.
  • DW Witness and Witness Cam turn a phone into a surveillance camera for a recorder.
  • thewitnessapp.com is a map of live events reported by people on the ground, and witnessapp.org is an early-stage research project.

Before you rely on it

  • Test it at home. Connect storage, record a minute and check the footage arrived.
  • Witness cannot hide the green or orange recording indicator on iPhone, and it always records sound. Recording sound is restricted in some places; see recording laws.
  • The footage lands in your own cloud account, so the security of that account matters. See does Witness encrypt video.
  • This is general information, not legal advice.

Questions

Is the Witness app free?

Yes. Witness Camera is free on iPhone and Android, with no account. Pro is optional and adds picture quality and extra outputs, at $1.99 a month or $29.99 once on the US App Store.

Is the Witness app available on iPhone and Android?

Yes. It needs iOS 17 or later on iPhone and Android 11 or later on Android. The Android app can be a release behind the iPhone app.

What is the Witness app for?

Recording police encounters, protests and immigration enforcement with the screen black, uploading footage to your own cloud as you film, and keeping it checkable with hashing and independent time-stamping.

Is this the same as the WITNESS charity or the game The Witness?

No. WITNESS is a human rights organisation and The Witness is a puzzle game. This is a separate app, listed as Witness: Camera Beyond Reach, and was called Witness Record until September 2026.

Does the Witness app have a server or collect my footage?

No. There is no Witness server. Footage goes to the cloud storage you connect, and the developer cannot see it.
