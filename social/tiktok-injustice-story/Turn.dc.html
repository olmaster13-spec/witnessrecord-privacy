What happens to the rest

For every recording that makes it out, there is another one that gets illegally deleted.

Baltimore, 2010
Officers deleted every video on a man’s phone after he filmed an arrest. The city paid $250,000.
Des Moines, 2018
Police seized a man’s phone and camera without a warrant and kept them twelve days. $125,000.
Philadelphia, 2021
An officer is accused in a lawsuit of deleting a man’s recording of his own arrest.
