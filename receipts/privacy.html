Privacy policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

In short: Receipts has no server. The developer never receives your receipts, your Google data or anything about how you use the app. Receipts are read on your iPhone and uploaded straight from your iPhone to your own Google Drive.

Who we are

Receipts is an iPhone app made by Olmo Parenti, an independent developer ("I", "me"). Questions about this policy go to olmaster13@gmail.com.

What the app accesses, and why

WhatWhyWhere it goes
Camera To scan receipts, only when you tap Scan Receipt. The scan is saved on your iPhone, then uploaded to your Google Drive.
Photos and files you choose To add screenshots, photos or PDFs. Apple's pickers give the app only the items you select, never your whole library. Uploaded to your Google Drive.
Google Drive: only files you use with Receipts To upload receipts into the folder you pick, create folders when you ask, and keep the Expense Report spreadsheet up to date. Your Google account.
Your Google account's name and email address To show which account is connected. Kept on your iPhone.

Google user data

When you sign in, Receipts asks Google for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission. With it, the app can reach only:

  • folders you pick in Google's folder picker,
  • folders and files the app creates for you (your receipts and the Expense Report spreadsheet).

It cannot see, read or change any other file in your Google Drive.

Receipts uses this access only to provide the features you see in the app: uploading receipts, writing their details (date, merchant, category, amount, currency, a link to the file and its name) into the Expense Report spreadsheet, and reading that spreadsheet back to show your totals. Google user data is:

  • never sent to the developer or to any third party,
  • never used for advertising, and never sold,
  • never used to train AI or machine-learning models,
  • never read by a human.

Receipts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Reading receipts happens on your iPhone

To find a receipt's total, date, merchant and category, Receipts uses Apple's on-device text recognition. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence it also uses Apple's on-device language model. This processing happens entirely on your iPhone. The receipt is not sent to the developer, to Apple or to any other service to be read.

What's stored on your iPhone

  • Your Google sign-in, kept in the iPhone Keychain so you stay signed in.
  • Receipts waiting to upload. Each receipt is kept only until it's safely in Google Drive and read, then the app deletes its copy.
  • A short list of recent receipts: a small thumbnail and the details read from each one.
  • Your settings: the folder you chose, the scan format, and the latest totals.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

What we don't do

  • No account with the developer, and no developer server.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking, and no third-party SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Third parties

Receipts talks directly to Google (sign-in, Google Drive and Google Sheets) to store your receipts and expense report in your account. Google handles that data under the Google Privacy Policy. Apple provides the on-device text recognition and language model.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Sign out in the app's Settings. This also revokes the app's access at Google.
  • Remove access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your receipts and expense reports in Google Drive like any other files.
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on your iPhone.

Because the developer holds no data about you, there's nothing for me to export or delete on my side.

Children

Receipts isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a change affects how Google user data is used, the app will say so before it takes effect.
