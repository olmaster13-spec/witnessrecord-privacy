Private by design

No account with us, no server, no tracking. Receipts are read on your iPhone and go straight to your Drive.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google and choose the account you want to use.
  2. Pick a folder in your Drive with Google's own folder picker.
  3. Scan or add receipts. Each one is uploaded, its total, date and merchant are read on your phone, and the folder's expense report updates.

How Receipts uses your Google data

Receipts asks Google for one permission: access to only the files and folders you use with the app (Google calls this drive.file). It can't see anything else in your Drive.

With that permission Receipts:

  • uploads your receipts into the folder you pick,
  • creates and updates an Expense Report spreadsheet in that folder,
  • reads your account's name and email address, only to show which account is connected.

Your data travels only between your iPhone and Google. Nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else, and Google data is never used for advertising. Full details are in the privacy policy.
