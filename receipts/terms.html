Check the amounts

Receipts reads totals, dates, merchants and categories automatically, and that reading can be wrong: a faded receipt, an unusual layout or a recognition mistake can produce a wrong value. The expense report is a convenience, not an accounting, tax or legal record. Check the details before you rely on them, for example before submitting an expense claim or a tax return. You can correct any receipt in the app.
