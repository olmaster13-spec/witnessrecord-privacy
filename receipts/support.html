Getting started

How do I choose where receipts go?

When you sign in, Google's folder picker opens. Pick any folder in your Drive. To change it later, tap Change on the home screen and choose Choose from Google Drive, or tap Create "Receipts" Folder to start a new one.

Can I use a different Google account?

Yes. Go to Settings → Switch Account, choose the account and pick a folder in it.

Why can't Receipts see my other folders?

On purpose. Receipts only has access to the folder you pick and the files it creates, never the rest of your Drive. Folders you've picked before show up under Your Receipts folders; any other folder is one tap away in Google's picker.

Scan Receipt says it needs a camera

Scanning uses your iPhone's camera, so it isn't available on devices without one (or in a simulator). Use Photos or Files to add receipts instead.

Expense reports

Where is my expense report?

In the same Google Drive folder as your receipts, as a Google Sheet called Expense Report. Tap Report on the home screen to open it. Its Summary tab shows totals by currency, category and month; Expenses has a row per receipt.

How do I keep separate reports for projects or months?

Use a folder for each one. Every folder gets its own Expense Report, and the home screen shows what's been spent in the current folder.

A total, date or merchant is wrong

Tap the receipt, fix the details and tap Save. Its row in the Expense Report updates. Receipts where no total was found show Add amount.

Can I edit the spreadsheet myself?

Yes. Change amounts, categories or delete rows in Google Sheets, and the totals in the app follow. Leave the hidden Receipt ID column in place: it's how the app finds each receipt's row.

Uploading

What happens if I have no signal?

Receipts saves each receipt on your iPhone first, then uploads it and updates the report as soon as you're back online.

A receipt says "Couldn't find the Drive folder"

The folder was deleted or moved to the trash in Google Drive. Tap Change, pick or create a folder, then tap Retry on the receipt.

I was asked to sign in again

Google ended the sign-in, for example because access was removed from your Google account. Sign in again; receipts that were waiting upload afterwards.

Privacy and your data

Does the developer see my receipts?

No. Receipts has no server. Receipts are read on your iPhone and go straight to your Google Drive. See the privacy policy.

How do I disconnect Receipts from Google?

Tap Settings → Sign Out in the app, or remove Receipts at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Your receipts and reports stay in your Drive until you delete them.
