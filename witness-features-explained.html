Witness features explained

What each feature does, how it works, and where its limits are.

Witness Camera is a free iPhone and Android app for recording safely and keeping footage checkable. Each page below explains one feature in detail, including what it cannot do. Written by the developer, so read it with that in mind.

Short answer. Witness records with the screen black, uploads a piece every 15 seconds to storage you own, hashes every piece with SHA-256, writes a chain-of-custody manifest and gets it independently time-stamped. Pro adds picture quality, burned-in time and location, a highlight cut and PDF evidence reports. It does not hide the recording indicator, always records sound and adds no encryption of its own.

Questions

What is Witness Camera?

A free iPhone and Android app for documenting situations where you may not be safe. It records with the screen black, uploads while recording to storage you own, and hashes and time-stamps the footage.

Which features are free?

Black-screen recording, upload during recording, SHA-256 hashing, the manifest, independent time-stamping, emergency-contact sharing and dual-camera recording at 1080p.

Which features need Pro?

4K, frame rates, HEVC 10-bit, ProRes, Apple Log or HDR, burned-in time and location, the combined two-camera video, the highlight cut and evidence packages.
